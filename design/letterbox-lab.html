Frameo constraints

The Android 6 tablet is fill-rate limited during transitions. A background that adds another photograph or a live filter can make animation less fluid.

Best balance

Deep charcoal is the safest default across every exposure. Darkened dominant color has more personality while remaining inexpensive, provided the sample is always clamped to a dark luminance.

Transition warning

The cropped and blurred duplicates add a second full-size image—the same kind of overdraw we just removed from the slideshow transition.
