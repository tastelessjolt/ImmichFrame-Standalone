OPTION 01 · AMBIENT

Dark bridge

Low
Sequential opacity

The old image settles into black, then the new one rises. Unhurried, neutral, and safest for the automatic slideshow.

cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)
OPTION 02 · AMBIENT

Exposure dip

Low
Dim · swap · lift

A dark overlay briefly lowers exposure; the photo swaps beneath it at the midpoint. Shorter and less theatrical than a full fade.

cubic-bezier(.45, 0, .2, 1)
OPTION 03 · NAVIGATION

Quiet cover

Medium
One moving plane

The old photo stays fixed while the new photo covers the final few percent. Natural acceleration makes the small movement feel decisive.

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
OPTION 04 · NAVIGATION

Clean push

Medium
Transform only

A conventional full-frame push. It communicates history direction clearly and avoids translucent blending, but moves two large textures.

cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)
OPTION 05 · GRAPHIC

Edge wipe

Medium
Animated clip

The new image is revealed by a precise moving edge. Crisp and contemporary, though Avalonia clipping should be profiled before adoption.

cubic-bezier(.76, 0, .24, 1)
OPTION 06 · EXPRESSIVE

Depth handoff

High
Scale + dissolve

A restrained zoom gives the dissolve depth. It is polished on capable hardware, but retains the current transition’s expensive overlap.

cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)
OPTION 07 · AMBIENT

Film drift

Low
Sequential + 1.8%

A tiny directional drift is split around a dark midpoint. It preserves the low-overlap benefit while feeling more tactile than a plain fade.

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
OPTION 08 · EXPRESSIVE

Soft iris

High
Circular clip

The next photograph opens from an off-centre focal point. Elegant and gallery-like, but animated circular clipping is a device-test candidate rather than a safe default.

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)
OPTION 09 · AMBIENT

Tint bridge

Low
Color field swap

A muted dominant-color field briefly fills the frame while the image swaps. It feels warmer than black and can reuse color already derived for the letterbox backdrop.

cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)
OPTION 10 · ACCESSIBILITY

Clean cut

Lowest
No interpolation

An immediate swap is the performance baseline and the correct reduced-motion fallback. Clean, reliable, and deliberately unanimated.

My first device test

Implement Dark bridge, Exposure dip, and Quiet cover behind a temporary transition selector. They cover ambient, fast, and directional use without filters.

Avoid on this Frameo

Live blur, large zooms, perspective rotation, and long crossfades. They increase texture sampling or overdraw exactly where this tablet struggles.

Timing range

Try 360–520 ms for ambient bridges and 260–380 ms for manual navigation. Longer motion makes any dropped frame easier to see.
