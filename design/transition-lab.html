ImmichFrame / transition study 01

Clean motion,
small hardware.

Four offline simulations for the 1280×800 Android 6 frame. The app itself still uses its existing crossfade; this page is only a decision tool for a possible follow-up.

OPTION 01

Fade through black

Recommended
Sequential opacity

The old photo fades out, the image swaps at black, then the new photo fades in. It feels intentional and avoids blending two detailed photos at the same instant.

  • Lowest blend load
  • One visible photo
  • Modern / calm
OPTION 02

Short crossfade

Familiar
Simultaneous opacity

The current visual language, shortened to roughly 180–300 ms. It reduces the time spent blending two textures, but still requires both photos every transition frame.

  • Medium blend load
  • Two visible photos
  • Least change
OPTION 03

Subtle directional slide

Expressive
Opacity + 2% shift

A very small horizontal shift gives next/previous a direction. It remains tasteful, but asks the GPU to blend and transform both photos, so it is not the safest frame default.

  • Medium–high load
  • Two photo layers
  • Clear direction
OPTION 04

Clean cut

Fastest
No animation

An immediate swap has no animation cost and is appropriate for reduced-motion mode. It is the most reliable option, though less gentle for an ambient photo frame.

  • Minimum load
  • One photo layer
  • Reduced motion

Best default for this Frameo

Fade through black at 300–450 ms. It hides the texture swap, avoids simultaneous image blending, and still reads as a deliberate transition rather than a performance concession.

What to avoid

Blur, zoom-heavy wipes, and long dissolves. They add filter or overdraw cost without improving the photograph, and frame drops remain visible for longer.
